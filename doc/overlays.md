# Overlays

Adaptive dialogs, bottom sheets, popovers, context menus, banners, toasts, and
tooltips.

## Key Widgets

* `AdwDialog` & `AdwAlertDialog` — Adaptive floating dialogs that automatically
  present as bottom sheets on narrow screens.
* `AdwAboutDialog`, `AdwPreferencesDialog`, & `AdwShortcutsDialog` — Ready-made
  GNOME standard dialogs.
* `AdwBottomSheet` — Interactive draggable bottom sheet with collapsed bar.
* `AdwPopover`, `AdwContextMenu`, & `AdwTooltip` — Anchored menus and bubbles.
* `AdwToastOverlay`, `AdwToast`, & `AdwBanner` — Non-blocking in-app
  notifications and top info banners.
