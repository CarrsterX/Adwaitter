# Controls

Interactive GNOME Adwaita input controls — buttons, switches, sliders, text
entries, dropdowns, toggle groups, and preference rows.

## Key Widgets

* `AdwButton`, `AdwButtonContent`, & `AdwSplitButton` — Standard, flat,
  suggested, destructive, pill, circular, and OSD buttons.
* `AdwSwitch`, `AdwCheckButton`, `AdwSlider`, & `AdwToggleGroup` — Toggles,
  checkboxes, scales, and segmented button groups.
* `AdwEntry`, `AdwSearchBar`, & `AdwDropdown` — Text fields, collapsible search
  bars, and popover dropdowns.
* `AdwButtonRow`, `AdwComboRow`, `AdwEntryRow`, `AdwSpinRow`, & `AdwSwitchRow`
  — Specialized preference rows for use inside `AdwPreferencesGroup`.
