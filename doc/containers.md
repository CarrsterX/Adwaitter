# Containers

Boxed lists, preference groups, cards, action rows, expander rows, and status
pages for organizing structured content.

## Key Widgets

* `AdwPreferencesPage` & `AdwPreferencesGroup` — Standard GNOME settings pages
  and boxed-list sections.
* `AdwActionRow` & `AdwExpanderRow` — Interactive list rows with prefix/suffix
  slots, `.property` styling, and expandable sub-rows.
* `AdwCard` — Elevated rounded surface container.
* `AdwStatusPage` — Empty-state, welcome, and error screens with large icons.
* `AdwLinkedBox` & `AdwOsdSurface` — Joined control groups and translucent
  on-screen display surfaces.
